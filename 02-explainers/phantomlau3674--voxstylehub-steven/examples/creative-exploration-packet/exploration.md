# Exploration

Two routes could carry this film. The chemistry route turns the receipt into a lab bench of paper test strips; the time-lapse route treats it as a tiny landscape slowly bleached by sun and time. Choose the lab bench because fading is a chemical story and the bench lets heat, light, and air become physical actors.

Thermal paper becomes the substrate for the whole film. A paper-cut lab moth called Doctor Dot guides the eye without carrying essential text. Heat is a vermilion iron-shaped cutout that first stamps letters into existence and later steals them back. Exact labels and dates remain code-native.

The sparse risk is the middle: repeating two more examples of fading would repeat the same picture. Change the spatial mechanism instead. Fold the receipt into a timeline accordion, with each fold representing another period of survival.

Motion carries meaning. The iron stamps to show activation; Doctor Dot peels a letter away to show the dye system letting go; the accordion settles flat only when the receipt becomes blank. Nothing moves merely to sparkle.
