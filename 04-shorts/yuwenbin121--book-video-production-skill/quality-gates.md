# Quality gates

## Factual and editorial

- Book identity and edition are correct.
- Claims map to recorded sources.
- Quotes, reviewer opinions, and reader reactions are attributed.
- Narration passes the editorial audit and target duration.

## Audio

- No clipping, severe noise, or abrupt edits.
- Chinese names and uncommon terms are pronounced correctly.
- Pace and pauses serve the meaning.
- Music does not mask speech.
- Music enters and exits cleanly, remains subordinate to narration, and is ducked when needed.
- Music source and usage rights are recorded.
- Final master, not draft audio, drives timing.

## Timing and captions

- Captions derive from the final-audio transcript.
- Caption changes follow spoken phrases closely.
- Lines remain readable long enough.
- No caption remains after its phrase without intentional hold.
- Scene boundaries follow semantic beats.

## Layout and typography

- Chinese glyphs render correctly.
- No text or cover is cropped, clipped, or stretched.
- Essential content stays inside platform-safe areas.
- Captions avoid UI overlays, logos, and focal objects.
- Cover title remains legible at phone size.
- A separate publishing cover exists and remains understandable as a small thumbnail.
- Cover copy is short, accurate, and consistent with the video.

## Visual assets

- Every image supports a specific narration beat.
- Era, setting, costume, objects, palette, and recurring characters remain coherent.
- Vintage scenery, cartoon, documentary, or symbolic treatment matches the individual book.
- No image is merely decorative filler when a more book-specific visual is available.
- Asset source, generation method, and usage rights are recorded.
- Published covers, screen adaptations, copyrighted characters, and living artists’ recognizable styles are not copied without authorization.

## Motion

- Motion has a reason: reveal, emphasis, hierarchy, or transition.
- No distracting continuous movement behind dense captions.
- Transitions do not hide important words.
- The opening establishes the hook quickly.

## Technical

- Preview/lint/inspect completed without unresolved errors.
- Output has expected dimensions, aspect ratio, FPS, duration, and audio.
- MP4 opens and plays through.
- Source project uses portable paths or documents required local assets.

Record pass/fail evidence. A gate remains false when a check was skipped.
